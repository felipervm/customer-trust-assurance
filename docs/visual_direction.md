# Trust in Review — visual direction and asset provenance

This independent portfolio case takes its cybersecurity-employer reference from [Chainguard](https://www.chainguard.dev/). The unrelated `chainguard.com` domain is not used as a design or company source.

The presentation combines charcoal, electric violet, lilac and a restrained lime accent with Manrope typography. The artwork illustrates traceability; the actual answers, source versions, checks and proposed review actions remain live HTML driven by the reproducible fixture data.

## Generated artwork

- Method: built-in ImageGen tool; original opaque-background image.
- Web asset: [trust-records.jpg](../site/assets/trust-records.jpg). The generated PNG master was converted to a delivery JPEG without changing its composition.
- Role: a conceptual source-to-answer provenance metaphor. It is not a screenshot, security attestation or depiction of Chainguard's internal systems.
- The separate author portrait was supplied by Felipe Mattos.

### Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: premium editorial hero artwork for an independent cybersecurity governance and customer assurance portfolio website. New original image, wide landscape 16:9. A tactile sculptural still life of three oversized matte lilac paper record slabs floating sequentially along one fine luminous electric-violet thread. Subtle blind-embossed horizontal lines suggest records but are not readable writing. One older offset sheet sits behind the sequence; the final paper slab is precisely aligned at a deliberate charcoal review aperture. Composition weighted to the RIGHT, with 40 percent calm near-black negative space on the LEFT for separately rendered HTML headline. Stage transitions from charcoal #0d151b on the left into saturated electric violet #6926ff on the right, soft lilac papers, one restrained acid-lime edge detail. Dramatic tactile side lighting, physically convincing paper thickness and texture, refined contemporary art direction, slight photographic grain, elegant depth and shadows, museum-quality CGI still life. This is a conceptual metaphor for source-to-answer provenance and human review, not a system screenshot, flowchart or security attestation. No text, letters, numbers, logos, company marks, shields, padlocks, glass lens, dashboards, circuitry, people, watermarks or UI. Opaque background.

## Typography and motion

Manrope is an open font embedded locally from its official Google Fonts distribution. The retained [SIL Open Font License](../site/assets/fonts/OFL.txt) accompanies the font file. The page does not call Google Fonts at runtime.

Scroll-led storytelling and manual controls support the same source → answer → human review narrative. Reduced-motion and small-screen layouts keep the content directly readable. A successful fixture check never changes the required human approval gate.
